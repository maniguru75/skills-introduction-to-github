## What we sell (one sentence)
Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) advisory, end-to-end implementation, audit and ISACA/IT certification training for regulated and non-regulated organisations across Oman, the GCC and EMEA.

## Service lines
1. **Advisory & consulting**: GRC strategy, gap assessments, risk frameworks, policy design.
2. **Implementation**: management systems and frameworks taken from paper to practice:
   - Management systems: ISMS (ISO/IEC 27001), BCMS (ISO 22301), AIMS (ISO/IEC 42001), PIMS (ISO/IEC 27701)
   - Frameworks & standards: NIST CSF, COBIT, ISO 31000, PCI DSS, SOC 2 Type I & II readiness
   - Regulatory: Oman Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)
   - Risk: ERM, RCSA (risk & control self-assessment), TPRM (third-party risk management)
   - Cybersecurity programme / project management
3. **Audit**: internal / IT audit, readiness and pre-certification audits.
4. **Training**: exam preparation for ISACA certifications (CISA, CISM, CRISC, CGEIT, CDPSE) and other IT skills.
   Independent trainer, NOT an ISACA-accredited training partner. Always say "exam preparation for CISA/CISM…".
   Never say "official", "accredited" or "authorised" ISACA training, and never use ISACA's logo or imply endorsement.

## Pains we solve, by segment
- **regulated_enterprise** (CISO / CRO / CIO / Internal Audit)
  1. New or changing regulator requirements with deadlines and limited in-house capacity.
  2. Audit findings that keep coming back because controls exist on paper but not in practice.
  3. Fragmented risk, compliance and security work with no single view for the board.
- **training_buyer** (L&D / HR / Procurement / Education)
  1. A skills gap in audit, risk and security roles that is hard to hire for locally.
  2. Certification targets (e.g. CISA/CISM) for staff, with no reliable delivery partner.
  3. Training spend with no clear link to job performance.
- **partner** (training providers, distributors, consultancies, forums)
  1. Client demand for GRC services or ISACA training they can't deliver in-house.
  2. Need for a credible delivery partner in Oman/GCC.

## Geo rationale (recommendation, please verify)
My suggested order: **Oman → Saudi Arabia & UAE → Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait → EU (stretch)**.
Why, as of my knowledge (laws and deadlines may have changed, so please check the official regulator sites):
- **Saudi Arabia**: the largest GCC economy, with active national cybersecurity regulation (NCA controls), central-bank (SAMA) cyber requirements, and a Personal Data Protection Law now in force.
- **UAE**: a federal data protection law, plus separate regimes in the DIFC and ADGM free zones; a large concentration of regional HQs.
- **Oman**: your home market, with its own Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree 6/2022). Local presence is a real advantage.
- **EU**: DORA (financial services) and NIS2 (critical sectors) drive strong GRC demand, but the market is more crowded and farther from your base.
